THE WINGS OF EPISTEME — MOBILE WEBSITE

Open index.html in a modern browser. The site is self-contained and uses local image assets.

Features: animated owl welcome, four themed wing books, 3D book-cover opening, page-turn animation, swipe navigation, and an interactive wand: open a book, tap “Pick up wand”, then draw a closed circle around the page to reveal its summary.

Note: Google Fonts is optional; local Georgia fallbacks are included.
