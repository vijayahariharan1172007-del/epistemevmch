EPISTEME & ITS WINGS

Rules, Regulations & Membership Guidelines

1. EPISTEME Membership

EPISTEME is the academic club responsible for organising and participating in intra-college and inter-college academic events. Students who join any of the four wings — VIVARA, COGNITIVA, REVELA, or ARTERA — will also be recognised as EPISTEME members, irrespective of whether they participate in EPISTEME’s academic events.

2. Understanding the Wings

The four wings function as distinct areas of extracurricular activity within EPISTEME: VIVARA: Learning and Intellectual Expression; COGNITIVA: Academic Learning and Innovation; REVELA: Research and Discovery; ARTERA: Creative Arts and Expression. Each wing has its own focus, activities, and designated Faculty In-Charge(s). Wing membership does not automatically require participation in EPISTEME-wide academic events, and EPISTEME membership does not prevent students from joining the wings.

3. Voluntary Participation in Academic Events

Although the wings primarily focus on their respective activities, EPISTEME may approach wing members for assistance during academic events or related programmes. Such participation is entirely voluntary and will take place only with the individual’s consent. Joining a wing does not automatically make a member responsible for assisting with every EPISTEME event.

4. Membership in Multiple Wings

Existing EPISTEME members may register for one or more of the four wings, subject to the respective registration or recruitment process and available capacity. Students joining a wing for the first time will also be recognised as EPISTEME members.

5. Election of Wing Leaders

Each wing will elect its Student In-Charge(s) through a transparent voting process. Students must register as members of the respective wing before the election to be eligible to contest. Eligible candidates may campaign before the election, and both existing EPISTEME members and newly registered members may contest. Leadership will be determined by the election results, not by how long a candidate has been an EPISTEME member. The elected candidate(s) will serve as the wing’s Student In-Charge(s).

6. Conduct, Responsibilities & Faculty Guidance

All members are expected to treat fellow students, faculty, organisers, and participants with respect; communicate responsibly; and fulfil agreed commitments. Wing activities must be planned in consultation with the designated Faculty In-Charge(s) and conducted in accordance with applicable institutional rules, permissions, and official instructions.

7. Official Communication

Members should follow official EPISTEME announcements and the communication channels designated by the organisers for information regarding registrations, activities, elections, responsibilities, and deadlines.

By proceeding with registration, you acknowledge that you have read and understood these guidelines and agree to abide by the applicable rules and official instructions.
