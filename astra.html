Academic Support & Development

ASTRA

Learn effectively. Reflect meaningfully. Grow together.

ASTRA is the EPISTEME initiative focused on academic support, learning strategies, reflection, and student development. It creates opportunities for students to share practical approaches to learning, manage academic demands, and navigate common study-related challenges in a respectful and supportive environment.

What does ASTRA offer?

  • Study-skills sessions and workshops on planning, revision, and active recall
  • Small-group discussions on managing study holidays and balancing learning tasks
  • Peer-sharing sessions on note-making, question practice, and exam preparation strategies
  • Reflection activities to set realistic goals and review progress
  • Guidance towards appropriate faculty, student-support, or institutional services when needed

Be a part of ASTRA

Share study strategies that have helped you, volunteer to organise sessions, prepare learning resources, or help collect anonymous feedback. Contribute by listening without judgement, respecting confidentiality, and recognising that different approaches work differently for different learners.
